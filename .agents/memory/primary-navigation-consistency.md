---
name: Primary navigation consistency
description: Product-wide expectation for applying Receiptify's approved visual language across primary navigation screens.
---

When refreshing Receiptify’s visual system, treat every primary bottom-navigation destination as one cohesive surface rather than migrating only analytics and receipt-management screens.

**Why:** The user specifically flagged Scan and Account when they retained the old treatment beside the refreshed Analytics, My Receipts, and Split screens; the resulting visual cliff made the app feel unfinished.

**How to apply:** Any future visual-system change should include a desktop and mobile consistency audit across all primary navigation routes, including dialogs opened from those routes.
